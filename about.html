About

Plenty of bachelorette and birthday groups book a hotel near the airport or a rental off Rivers Avenue and then realize they don't know the area. North Charleston has the region's clubs and a fun stretch of bars around Park Circle, but very little for anyone looking for male entertainment.

Local performers we work with include Michael At Your Place, a Charleston-based entertainer who's a regular at North Charleston bachelorette parties.

Want us to sort out a private show for your group? Everything you need is on the private party page. Guests must be 18 or older.
