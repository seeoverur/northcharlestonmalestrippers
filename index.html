North Charleston: Clubs, Revues & Private Dancers

North Charleston is where the Lowcountry's gentlemen's clubs actually are. This guide lists them, along with the touring male revues, private dancers, and Park Circle bars worth knowing about.

Rather keep the party at the house? Call or text to see if your date is open.

For the bride's side

Revue Nights

If you're hoping for a male club to match the ones below, there isn't one anywhere around Charleston, and revue nights tend to pop up and disappear. Magic Men Live and iCandy Male Revue tour the Carolinas a few times a year, and HonkyTonk Saloon over in Ladson, about 15 minutes up the road, sometimes hosts one. When the calendar doesn't cooperate, a private dancer is the reliable option.

At your place

Private Dancers

For a private show, Michael At Your Place is a common pick. He's based in Charleston, charges $200 for a show of up to 90 minutes, and takes payment on arrival by cash, Venmo, or Cash App. His regular areas are Charleston, Mount Pleasant, Folly Beach, and Isle of Palms, he does brunch or late night, and last-minute dates are welcome. North Charleston is about 20 minutes from downtown, and he makes the trip for parties here.

The groom's side

Gentlemen's Clubs & Female Dancers

G Club Gentlemen's Club sits on Peppermill Parkway toward the Ladson end of town, and Gen X Cabaret keeps 2 p.m. to 2 a.m. hours every day with a full bar and VIP areas. Downtown's King Street Cabaret, Secrets Cabaret, and Thee Southern Belle are 15 to 20 minutes south. Bachelor groups who'd rather stay in can book a female dancer for the house through local entertainment services.

Park Circle Bars

Park Circle is the walkable part of town. DIG in the Park has live music and stays open until 2 a.m., Sissy Bar does craft cocktails, and Madra Rua is the neighborhood Irish pub. Tattooed Moose has bands a few nights a week, and Commonhouse Aleworks and Holy City Brewing cover beer, food, and trivia. Firefly Distillery closes in the early evening, so make it an afternoon stop. Heading up I-26 for part of the weekend? Try the Summerville nightlife guide, or if the trip ends at the beach, see Folly Beach after dark.
